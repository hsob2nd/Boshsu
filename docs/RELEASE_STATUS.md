# Release status and validation

Current candidate: **Boshsu 1.3 RC1** (`v1.3.0-rc.1`), Windows x64. This is a feature-frozen optimization candidate. It is not a blanket correctness, all-hardware or zero-spike certificate.

The [engineering report](STABLE_REMASTERED_V13_REPORT.md), [performance comparison](STABLE_REMASTERED_V13_PERFORMANCE.md), [resource report](STABLE_REMASTERED_V13_RESOURCES.md), [regression report](STABLE_REMASTERED_V13_REGRESSION.md) and [181-section qualification ledger](STABLE_REMASTERED_V13_REQUIREMENTS.md) distinguish completed checks from retained failures and unqualified areas.

Expanded PP golden states and qualified assist traces match exactly. Existing PP accuracy/HD edge cases and a captured dense-map one-judgment replay mismatch also reproduce on sealed RC6; they remain unresolved. Mean recorded frame work is similar/slightly higher in the matched runs; rare long frames remain. No general FPS/memory win or improved tails are certified.

Pause, synthetic raw-input/F9, skin/resolution chrome, actual Windows file-picker ownership and copied-installation rollback/preservation checks passed. A fresh Git-free native/managed source build passed on this development PC and the qualified model rebuilt byte-identically. Installed compiler inputs were reused; full clean-machine/Linux/whole-host reproducibility is not certified.

Live authentication/submission, stable score equivalence/legacy `.osr` conversion, non-Standard modes, other hardware, physical-device feel, real 4K/Windows DPI matrices and Linux launch remain unqualified. Hosted workflow results must be checked in Actions separately from local tests.

Clean packages exclude player profiles, scores, avatars, settings, maps, custom skins, replays and telemetry. The owner-authorized qualified model and calculator modules are shared. Detailed diagnostics default Off with one-session retention and hard caps; saved player content is independent of diagnostic quotas. Running personal installations are not silently replaced.

GPL/MIT/OFL and dependency notices remain. The historical Bosh PP implementation is prebuilt and its source/provenance is unavailable. Supplied artwork/resource/audio/helper redistribution rights are unresolved; source availability, public access and prerelease labels do not clear them.
